## Backdrops, bulbs and lighting
All backdrops, posters, bulbs, speakers, spotlights and particle textures are original and drawn in code
(`js/ui/Backdrop.js`, `BulbRing.js`, `Speaker.js`, `Spotlights.js`, `Effects.js`). No real film posters are used.

## Music and sound
All four music styles and all and sound effects are original and synthesised in code (`js/audio/`).
No recordings or film songs are included.

## Song titles
Song titles and film names are used only as puzzle answers. The game does not include
any lyrics, audio or artwork from the films. "Listen on YouTube" links open a YouTube search.
